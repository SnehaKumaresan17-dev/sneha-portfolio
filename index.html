About

A designer who ships, not just mocks up.

I'm a fresher with a BCA, based in Tiruvallur, Chennai, open to full-time design roles. I've been moving deeper into UI/UX — research, wireframing, prototyping, and visual design in Figma — with growing interest in data analytics as well.
